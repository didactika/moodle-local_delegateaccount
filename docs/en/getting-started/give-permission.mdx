---
title: Give people permission
summary: Create the role that lets people use delegations, and assign it.
order: 11
---

The plugin adds a capability, `local/delegateaccount:use` (**Log in as a delegated account**). Nobody has it by default, so you give it through a role of your own.

<Steps>
1. Go to **Site administration > Users > Permissions > Define roles** and add a new role, for example *Delegated account user*.
2. Select **System** as the only context type and allow `local/delegateaccount:use`.
3. Go to **Site administration > Users > Permissions > Assign system roles**, open the role and give it to the people who should be able to use delegations.
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="asset:roles-assign.webp" alt="The Assign system roles page with three users in the role Delegated account user" caption="The role assigned to three people. They are now the authorised users the plugin lists." url="/admin/roles/assign.php" />

Until a role grants the capability, the management page reminds you of these two steps with links to the two pages.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not grant `local/delegateaccount:use` to the **Authenticated user** role. Every user would then count as authorised.

The people who hold the role are the **authorised users**: the ones you can create delegations for. [Create your first delegation](page:getting-started/quick-start).
