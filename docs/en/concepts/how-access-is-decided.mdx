Delegate Account does not open accounts by itself: it decides whether Moodle's own **Log in as** may be used for a given pair of users at a given moment.

## Two conditions

Two things must be true for someone to open an account:

<FeatureGrid columns={2}>
  <Feature icon="key-rounded" title="They hold the permission">They have `local/delegateaccount:use` in the system context, or they are a site administrator.</Feature>
  <Feature icon="swap-horiz-rounded" title="A delegation exists">An administrator created a delegation from them to that account, and it is currently active.</Feature>
</FeatureGrid>

## The life of a delegation

Each delegation moves through four statuses, and the status is calculated from its dates, not stored.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <Badge text="Scheduled" variant="note" /> | The start date has not been reached yet |
| <Badge text="Active" variant="success" /> | The account can be opened now |
| <Badge text="Expired" variant="caution" /> | The end date has passed |
| <Badge text="Revoked" variant="danger" /> | An administrator ended it. Revoked delegations are kept for the record |

To give access again after a delegation expires, [edit the expired delegation](page:guides/manage-delegations) instead of creating a new one.

## When a delegated session ends

Access is checked when the account is opened and again on every page while the delegated session lasts. The session ends on the next page if:

- the delegation is revoked or expires;
- the person loses the permission;
- the account is suspended or deleted;
- the account becomes a site administrator while that is protected (see [the security model](page:concepts/security-model)).

Nothing depends on cron: start and end dates are checked each time, so delegations start and stop on time.
