People with active delegations see **Delegated accounts** in their user menu. Choosing an account opens it through Moodle's **Log in as** and goes to the Dashboard of that account.

<Screenshot src="asset:user-menu-accounts.webp" alt="The user menu open on Delegated accounts, with two accounts to choose" caption="The user menu lists the accounts the person may open." />

When someone has more accounts than the menu shows, **View all delegated accounts** opens the **My delegated accounts** page, with a card for each account, until when it can be opened and the **Open account** button.

<Screenshot src="asset:my-accounts.webp" alt="The My delegated accounts page with two cards" caption="My delegated accounts: each card has an Open account button." url="/local/delegateaccount/pages/accounts.php" />

## Stop using the account

To stop working as the other account, log out. A delegated account cannot open another delegated account: log out first.

> [!NOTE]
> Everything done in the account is recorded in the site log under both names. If the delegation ends or is revoked while the account is in use, the session closes on the next page and the person must log in again.
