Two guided tours for Moodle's User Tours are imported automatically when the plugin is installed or upgraded. They are written for the people who use delegated accounts, not for administrators.

<Screenshot src="asset:tour-my-accounts.webp" alt="The first step of the tour of the My delegated accounts page" caption="The tour of the My delegated accounts page: step 1 of 5." url="/local/delegateaccount/pages/accounts.php" />

Each tour is shown only to people who currently have an active delegation, because it depends on the **Delegated accounts** entry in the user menu, and each person sees it once, until they finish or skip it.

| Tour | Where it appears | What it explains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Using a delegated account | Dashboard and My courses | Where to find **Delegated accounts** in the user menu and how to open an account. |
| My delegated accounts | **My delegated accounts** page | The cards of the accounts, until when each can be opened and the **Open account** button. |

A tour that already exists, identified by its name, is never overwritten, so your edits are kept. Tours can be edited, translated or disabled under **Site administration > Appearance > User tours**. If you delete a tour and want it back, select **Import tour** on that page and choose the tour file of the plugin.
